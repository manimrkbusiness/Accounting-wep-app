## Launch tasks & external submissions

Remaining pre-launch work — and anything that must be submitted to Google or other outside
platforms (sitemap, Google Search Console verification, Bing, etc.) — is tracked in
[`docs/launch-tasks/`](docs/launch-tasks/README.md).

When the user asks what's left to launch, or for "the reports / things to submit to Google and
other platforms", read `docs/launch-tasks/README.md` (and its linked files) and tell them exactly
what's pending and the steps — don't answer from memory. Keep the status in that folder up to date
as tasks are completed.
